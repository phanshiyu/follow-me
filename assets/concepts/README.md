# Logo concepts

- `a.svg`: a mama duck with googly mismatched eyes leading ducklings labelled `a1f`, `b2e` and `wip`; stupid because the `wip` duckling has fallen over with its legs in the air and nobody stopped.
- `b.svg`: a sweaty cyan commit blob with one huge eye and its tongue out waddling down a branch after a smug red blob wearing a party hat labelled `HEAD`; stupid because the follower is clearly not keeping up and is very happy about it.
- `c.svg`: a stick figure with mismatched eyes and tongue out dragging a sack labelled `47` along the branch while commits leak out the back; stupid because one commit is peeking out of the sack looking confused.
